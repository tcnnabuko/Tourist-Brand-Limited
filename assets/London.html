Overview

London is the capital of England, the largest city of the country which is located at United Kingdom in Europe. It can also be described as the largest city of the United Kingdom and the third largest in Europe with a population of 15.4 million people. It stands as a global hub, well-known for it's cultural, financial, educational, technological, tourist and entertainment values. London consists of over 50 universities and colleges which accumulate about 500000 students as of 2023. It's diverse culture encompass over 300 people. Landmarks include the Tower of London, Westminster Abbey, Buckingham Palace, the London Eye, Piccadilly Circus, St Paul's Cathedral, Tower Bridge and Trafalgar Square. [1] The city has the most museums, art galleries, libraries and cultural venues in the UK, including the British Museum, the National Gallery, the Natural History Museum, Tate Modern, the British Library and numerous West End theatres.
